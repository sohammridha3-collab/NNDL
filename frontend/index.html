Architecture Highlights

Engineered for Fairness and Precision

A multi-stage pipeline designed to evaluate real technical merit and dismantle algorithmic discrimination.

Demographic Blindfolding

Personal identifiers (names, gender pronouns, contact addresses, institutional pedigrees) are neutralized before entering vector pipelines, ensuring decisions rest solely on demonstrated capabilities.

770-Feature Neural Classifier

Combines dense SentenceTransformer embeddings, element-wise vectors, and canonical skill coverage into a trained PyTorch multilayer perceptron calibrated for precision and recall.

Counterfactual Fairness Audit

Synthetic variation testing checks model invariance across demographic profiles, proving score parity and guarding against algorithmic bias.

Actionable Skill Gap Insights

Provides candidates and recruiters with exact skill coverage percentages, matched requirements, and specific missing competencies for targeted upskilling.

Transparent Workflow

How Candidate Matching Works

A clear three-stage process from document ingestion to calibrated recommendation.

1

Ingestion & Privacy Sanitization

Resumes are parsed to extract structured skill ontologies and work experience. Sensitive demographic indicators are masked automatically to protect candidate privacy.

2

Semantic Vector Fusion

Deep SentenceTransformer embeddings encode contextual nuance, while canonical skill extractors quantify hard-skill overlap between resume competencies and job specifications.

3

Calibrated Scoring & Decision

The PyTorch MatchNetwork evaluates all 770 dimensions, outputting a strong-match compatibility score, semantic similarity ratio, and a complete skill breakdown.
